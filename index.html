Marvel Master Watchlist

A guided watchlist for the MCU and major connected or separate Marvel franchises, in a recommended viewing order.

About this page

This is a personal, fan-made guide to watching the Marvel Cinematic Universe and related Marvel film/TV franchises in a sensible order — not an official Marvel, Disney or Marvel Studios product.

The descriptions, Essential/Recommended/Optional priority tags and viewing-order notes were entirely AI-generated. Treat them as a helpful starting point, not gospel — there's bound to be the odd mistake or debatable call, so take anything that seems off with a pinch of salt rather than as fact.

How your progress is saved

Your ticks and notes save automatically in this browser's storage as you go — no account needed. They'll still be there next time you open this exact page in this same browser, on this same device.

This can be lost if you clear this browser's site data, use a private/incognito window, or open the page in a different browser or on a different device — each keeps its own separate copy, with nothing synced between them.

Download a copy saves a standalone HTML file with your current progress built in — a good backup, a way to move your progress somewhere else, or a fully offline copy that needs no browser storage at all. In a browser that supports it (e.g. Chrome, Edge), the first click asks you to pick your existing watchlist file and immediately overwrites it with your current progress; every click after that writes straight back into that same file without asking again — so it's genuinely keeping one file up to date, not piling up copies. In other browsers (e.g. Safari), it downloads a brand-new file each time, and you'll need to manually replace your old one with it to keep your progress.

Scope note: “All Marvel” spans decades of live-action films, television, animation, shorts and one-off adaptations. This is a substantial curated guide to the major live-action Marvel franchises, MCU releases and particularly relevant animation—not an exhaustive catalogue of every vintage cartoon, short, international production or direct-to-video title.

Continuity can be messy: some Marvel Television shows began as MCU-adjacent, and some Marvel Studios animation explores alternate universes rather than the main MCU timeline. “MCU” labels here mean Marvel Studios' main saga or its officially associated multiverse projects; “Non-MCU” means a separate original franchise/continuity.
